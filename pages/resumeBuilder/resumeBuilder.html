Step 2 of 6 · Optional

Disabilities and support

Which apply to you? You can choose both, or skip this step.

Your choices will not appear on your resume.

Choose any that apply

For example: a ramp, help with reading, or help returning to work.

No diagnosis or details about your incarceration are needed.

Step 3 of 6 · Experience is optional

Your experience counts

Paid jobs, work while incarcerated, volunteering, caregiving, and personal projects all count.

I have experience to add

Start with one thing you have done. It does not have to be a paid job.

I have no experience yet

That’s okay. You can build your resume from your education and skills.

You do not need to explain gaps in work.

Step 3 of 6 · Add one experience

Tell us what you did

Paid and unpaid experience both count. Start with one role or activity.

For work while incarcerated, a general description is enough.

For example: prepared meals, organized supplies, or cared for a family member.

No exact dates? Leave them blank. You do not need to explain gaps.

Step 4 of 6 · Education is optional

Learning at any level counts

Primary school, unfinished schooling, adult classes, and job training all count. Learning while incarcerated counts too.

I have schooling or training to add

Add one school, class, or program. You do not have to have finished it.

Nothing to add, or prefer not to share?

No formal schooling? That’s okay. You can leave education off your resume and focus on your skills.

Step 4 of 6 · Add one learning experience

Add schooling or training

Finished or not, it counts. Share only the details you know.

A general program name is fine. You do not need to name a prison.

An approximate year is fine. Leave it blank if you do not know.

Step 5 of 6 · Skills are optional

What can you do?

Skills can come from everyday life, caring for others, work, or training. Choose only what fits you.

Separate skills with a comma.

Step 6 of 6 · Review

Make sure it feels like you

Check what you want to share. Use Edit to return to any section.

Your disability and support answers stay off your resume.
